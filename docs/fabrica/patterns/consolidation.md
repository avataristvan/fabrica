# Consolidation

**Status: probation**

## Problem

A record that only grows becomes dead weight: duplicates, outdated statements, cues that no longer fit.

## Forces

- Deleting loses reasons.
- Never deleting buries them.

## Solution

Now and then the record is consolidated: records are linked, what is outdated is superseded, what carries no reason is deleted, duplicates are merged, and cues are sharpened. A tool may propose changes; only the Archivist applies them.

## Status

Probation. The formal Archivist evaluation failed (11 of 33 expected links, no predecessors found, a duplicate not flagged). It was closed without a rerun and accepted on real use, after a consolidation pass over about 245 records.
