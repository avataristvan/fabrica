# Wisdom, not only rules

**Status: hypothesis**

## Problem

A rule says what to do, not whom it affects. A rule that works technically can still hurt people.

## Forces

- More fields make records heavier.
- Most rules affect no one beyond the work.

## Solution

An optional `Affected:` line on records where people bear the consequences: who is affected, and how. It is written only where it applies.

## Status

Hypothesis. Not implemented: the record format does not know the field, and no record carries it.

## Evidence

- [`epistemology_framework.md`](../../sources/epistemology_framework.md): the first account of the idea, with an `Affected:` field for anti-patterns that involve people.
- [Manifesto draft](../../sources/fabrica_manifesto_draft_2026_10_10.md), principle 13.
