Decision: Domain/business decisions should be captured immediately when they surface during coding, not deferred to end-of-session reflection.
Why: /reflect's Step 3 asks for learnings only at session end, which loses knowledge surfaced mid-session and never revisited. Semantic understanding of what IS a domain decision requires immediate agent judgment during the moment of discovery.
Scope: All development sessions involving ai-mem knowledge capture. Use type='domain' (structure: Decision: / Why: / Scope:) for these entries specifically, distinct from type='feedback' (agent corrections, still at session end) and type='pattern'/'anti-pattern' (coding technique, not business rationale).
