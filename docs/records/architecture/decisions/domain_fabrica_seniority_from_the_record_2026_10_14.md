---

Decision: In Fabrica, seniority comes from the record itself, not from the agent or the model: like the lodge book of a cathedral workshop (Bauhuette), carried from generation to generation of builders, the record is carried from session to session. This sharpens the Junior -> Senior claim (competence grows from organised experience; docs/sources/epistemology_framework.md "The Junior -> Senior Effect", manifesto principle 15 in docs/sources/fabrica_manifesto_draft_2026_10_10.md): the carrier of seniority is the documentation passed on across sessions. The effect is still unmeasured (hypothesis).
Why: The agent starts every session without experience; what persists is the record, so competence placed in a model, ranker or per-agent profile would not travel.
Scope: Fabrica framing (docs/fabrica/core.md section "Fabrica and Kaizen", README.md section Fabrica). Decided by the user 2026-10-14.
