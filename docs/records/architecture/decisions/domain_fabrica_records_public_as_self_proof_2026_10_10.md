Decision: Records about the framework itself are published with Fabrica. Fabrica was built with Fabrica: its own lodge book (charters, handoffs, evidence, superseded decisions, corrections) is the proof that the framework works (Navigator, 2026-10-10: 'it would prove itself'). Handoffs belong to it again: published pseudonymised after the user's review, see [domain_handoffs_may_be_published_pseudonymised_after_review_2026_11_01](../../records/decisions/domain_handoffs_may_be_published_pseudonymised_after_review_2026_11_01.md) (which superseded the same-day local-only decision). The line of thought is the valuable public part. Private details are not. The Navigator reads and checks their own entries, or has them checked. Possibly all of them are useful publicly. Where a record or source touches private matters, it is generalised or pseudonymised in place, not withheld. Examples: the person becomes 'the Navigator', third parties become a description of their role (e.g. 'an external installation with an auto-updating agent'), absolute home paths become area-relative paths. This is a deliberate, explicit exception to 'evidence never changes' ([domain_three_kinds_of_knowledge_age_differently_2026_10_10](domain_three_kinds_of_knowledge_age_differently_2026_10_10.md)). For a public repo, protecting people outweighs the immutability of evidence, so no separate private original is kept. Each such change is noted in the record ('pseudonymised for publication, <date>'). Records stay with the topic they describe. Update 2026-10-14 (user): Fabrica lives in the ai-mem repo under docs/fabrica/ and its records live in ai-mem's area, next to the plugin-specific ones (MCP removal, doctor, docs and sources, adopting hand-written records, todos, the dev-team integration). This replaces the earlier plan that Fabrica's records would sit in an area and repo of their own.
Why: A framework of practices is proven by its practice, not by its description. This narrows the decision of 2026-10-04 (commit 380df16), which kept docs/records/ out of the public ai-mem repo as the Navigator's private decision graph: private details stay private, the reasoning becomes public. Pseudonymising protects third parties (manifesto principles 'The record is tended' and 'Wisdom, not only rules', in the draft docs/sources/fabrica_manifesto_draft_2026_10_10.md; the principles are becoming patterns under docs/fabrica/patterns/, see [domain_fabrica_practice_not_process_pattern_language_2026_10_14](domain_fabrica_practice_not_process_pattern_language_2026_10_14.md)) without losing the why. Breaking a rule consciously and saying so is itself a Fabrica practice, part of the data hygiene principle ('The record is tended'): an explicit exception with a reason, not silent drift.
Scope: the archivist proposes generalised versions and the Navigator approves before publishing. The question left open on 2026-10-10, whether Fabrica becomes its own public git repo, is answered on 2026-10-14: one public repo (ai-mem); a rename of the repo to fabrica is deferred.
