Decision: Fabrica is positioned by its kinship with Kaizen (Toyota Production System): hansei = Taskly, yokoten = librarian and areas (sideways spread of a lesson), standard work = decisions with reasons, superseded not deleted, 5 Whys = the why travels with the work, A3 = the record format, PDCA = charter / work / review against the record / Taskly+consolidation. Like Kaizen it runs alongside the work. The README uses this as its hook ("If you know Lean, you know its shape"), framed as an analogy of shape, not a promise of effect.
Why: At Toyota experience lives in people's heads; agents start each session without one, so Fabrica keeps explicitly what a human team carries implicitly.
