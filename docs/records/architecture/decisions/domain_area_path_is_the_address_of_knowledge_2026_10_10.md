Decision: Knowledge is addressed by the folder of its area: the nearest ancestor with docs/records/. A CLAUDE.md is not required (Navigator, 2026-10-10). It helps where sessions start in the area (charter, routing), but plays no part in addressing. Neither a collection name nor a git repo is the address. There is no catch-all level and no personal area. Every record goes to the area of its topic. Cross-cutting topics get an area of their own: a sibling area ai-architecture for how the Navigator works with AI. Machine and tooling facts (the environment area) live in the workspace root area, the docs/records/ of the folder that holds all areas (Navigator, 2026-10-11, D5 changed; first planned as a separate environment area). Whose knowledge it is goes into the record as `perspective:` (domain_perspective_is_a_lens_not_a_location_2026_10_10). How areas relate is a context map, not a hierarchy of levels. Edges point at ids. References across areas are relative paths or area names, never absolute paths. The old collections (`workspace`, `global`, `subagent.*`) dissolve into areas.
Why: The Navigator decided in the ai-architecture area that collection names derived from the folder hierarchy go away, records live in the area's docs/records/, and they stay readable without the librarian (domain_records_live_in_folder_docs_not_collection_hierarchy_2026_10_10 there). Areas must be movable without a migration, and some areas are not git repos. The Navigator also wants project knowledge kept out of a shared level, while cross-cutting questions belong in their own area. CLAUDE.md is optional because records-only folders (the records home, fresh areas) are never worked in, but must still be addressable. D5 (2026-10-11): the area lookup bubbles up from a path and ends at the workspace root, so the environment records sit exactly where that walk ends. That is why the lookup needs no sibling sweep. The workspace root is still not a catch-all level: only machine and tooling facts go there, everything else goes to its topic's area.
Scope: ai-mem's `mem` CLI (`mem context` finds the area by path), the navigation reference, the archivist (picks an area by topic). Open: how the session-start hook finds areas once the workspace-root variable is no longer needed for naming. Update 2026-10-14 (user): Fabrica no longer has an area of its own; its records live in ai-mem's area (see domain_framework_named_fabrica_2026_10_10).
