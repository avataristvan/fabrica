Decision: Records distinguish three kinds of knowledge that age differently. State (FOCUS.md, handoffs) is overwritten. Decisions with their reasons (records) are only added to, and outdated ones are superseded, not deleted. Evidence (docs/sources/<slug>.md, raw material such as transcripts in docs/sources/<slug>/) stays unchanged. The name and place were agreed on 2026-10-10. The sources/ and transcripts/ folders that had arisen ad hoc in the interview-notes project and in another project's area move there. The archivist never edits evidence. A learning that rests on a source points to it (Evidence: path, or derived_from when the source is a record).
Why: Mixing them lets state overwrite reasons, or lets interpretation creep into evidence. Separating what a source says from what we infer keeps claims checkable, which is the Navigator's epistemic working style ([domain_two_layer_knowledge_base_2026_10_10](../../../../../briefing-interviewer/docs/records/general/decisions/domain_two_layer_knowledge_base_2026_10_10.md) in the interview-notes project; the ai-architecture handoff of 2026-10-10, conclusion 1).
Scope: Navigation reference, archivist rules, the record format (evidence as a recognised layer next to docs/records/ and docs/reference/).
