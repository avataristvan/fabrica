Rule: Separate three levels of work in an ai-mem session. (1) Method: the Fabrica framework, including the human-agent role definitions -> ai-mem's area (Fabrica lives in the ai-mem repo under docs/fabrica/; decision of 2026-10-14, user, replacing the earlier separate fabrica area). (2) Build: building ai-mem, the tool, and the agent roles it complements the method with (plugin code, librarian, archivist, anchor-lint, skills, docs, evals, its own design and history) -> ai-mem's area as well. (3) Use: using ai-mem as the tool to configure the user's environment (wiring the user's own global skills or commands into the handoff protocol, dev-team and its worktree practice, /ai-mem:setup on a project, the user's records stores and other projects' areas, machine and tooling facts) -> the environment's area (the dev-team home or general-engineering area for team practice, a global or workspace-level area for machine and user-level facts, or the configured project's area), even when the session runs in the ai-mem repo. Archivist: choose the area by the level and topic of the work, not by the session's repo.
No edge crosses between the build/method area and the use level. A record moved out of ai-mem keeps no edge to an ai-mem record, and no ai-mem record keeps an edge to it; name the other side by id in plain text instead. A record that carries both levels is split, and each half stays on its side. Before writing any edge, check the level of both ends; the back-reference rule has no exception for this. Edges from the use level into the method level (ai-mem) are lineage and stay allowed.
When: Integrating candidates from an ai-mem session that configured the user's environment (e.g. rewiring the persona skills or dev-team) rather than building ai-mem, and whenever an edge is about to connect an ai-mem record with a use record.
Why: Using the tool to configure an environment is a different level from building the tool (user, 2026-10-13). Two persona todos were filed in ai-mem on 2026-10-11, had to be closed again, and their names went public with ai-mem's history. Edges between the levels mix them again (user, 2026-10-13). Editing the user's own config at this level is legitimate when asked; the "never edit" rule in domain_agents_learn_environment_never_edit_external_2026_10_11 concerns external third-party agents and does not apply. If the target area is unclear or not writable, report the candidate to the caller instead of filing it in ai-mem.
