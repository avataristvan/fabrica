---

Decision: "Out of scope" is not a backlog section. A dropped item leaves the backlog. When the "no" is worth knowing later (the idea could come back), its reason is a decision record. Not every drop is a decision.
Why: A won't-do list in the backlog is a second place for next steps' opposite and rots like any status list; the reason, if it matters, belongs with the decisions, which are searched by situation.
Scope: BACKLOG.md and /reflect (skills/reflect/SKILL.md Step 4 and Step 6).
