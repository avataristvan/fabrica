Rule: Rules that must act before an action (a pre-action check) are routed to CLAUDE.md/docs, not left to records; a record's cue names the pre-action moment ("before you ..."), not the topic.
When: Writing a cue, or choosing between a record and CLAUDE.md for a rule.
Why: A galaxy-tab-a-2016 session reported a recall gap for before-action rules: records are poorly recalled through the topic.
